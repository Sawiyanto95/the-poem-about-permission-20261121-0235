A short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given. The program writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The poem

> The door was locked from my side.
> I knocked once, and my own hand
> answered.
>
> Permission was never a gift.
> It was the sound of asking —
> me, letting myself in.
